# Appointment hours

Jasper Plumbing House offers one-hour appointments Monday through Saturday from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM Eastern Time. Appointment starts are on the hour from 8:00 AM through 5:00 PM; the 5:00 PM slot ends at 6:00 PM. Sunday appointments are not offered.

These are the operating hours, not a promise that every slot is open. The connected Calendar determines whether a particular in-hours slot is free. The current booking workflow checks a slot only as part of a customer-authorized booking request; if available, it saves the booking and sends a confirmation email. Do not call it merely to answer an hours or availability-only question. For a time outside the stated window, explain the schedule and ask the visitor to choose an eligible start time before proceeding.
